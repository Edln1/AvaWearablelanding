A few things you might wonder.

01What is Ava?

Ava is the personal assistant inside Synapses. Talk naturally, work through an idea in chat, and bring useful context into the conversation.

02Can I look around before signing up?

Yes. Explore as a guest to see the interface and recorded previews. An account is needed for your own conversations and connected services.

03What can I ask for?

Start with a question, a draft, a plan, or something you want to think through. Voice, memory, Gmail, calendar, and other tools become useful when they fit the task and are available to your account.

04Do I need to connect my Google account?

You can begin without connecting Google. Gmail and Calendar connections add the context you choose to authorize when you are ready to use those features.

05Does Ava remember things?

Synapses includes memory so useful preferences and context can help future conversations. You can review memory in the app rather than repeat everything each time.

06What happens when I get started?

You can create an account, sign in to an existing one, or enter the guest preview. The welcome flow introduces Ava and lets you choose where to begin.
